Kerala IT guide

Official websites for Kerala’s IT parks, park job portals, Tech Keralam, startup support, skills programmes and cyber safety. Every link below goes to the organisation’s own site, not to InfoparkDaily.

Kerala IT parks

Government and private IT parks with their own official sites. Start here to check a company’s address or a park’s facilities.

  • Infopark Kerala — infopark.in. Kochi (Kakkanad), plus Infopark’s other campuses; company list and the official vacancy board.
  • Technopark — technopark.org. Thiruvananthapuram campus, companies and infrastructure.
  • Cyberpark Kozhikode — cyberparks.in. Government IT park for north Kerala; facilities and openings.
  • SmartCity Kochi — smartcity-kochi.in. IT township in Kakkanad; company updates and job openings.
  • UL CyberPark — ulcyberpark.com. IT park in Kozhikode; jobs page for its companies.
  • KINFRA — kinfra.org. Kerala Industrial Infrastructure Development Corporation parks.

Official job portals

These are the boards the parks themselves run. A job here still needs checking with the company before you share documents.

  • Infopark jobs — infopark.in/companies-job. Vacancies posted by Infopark companies, with deadlines.
  • Technopark jobs — technopark.in/job-search. Vacancies posted by Technopark companies.
  • Cyberpark careers — cyberparks.in/careers. Openings at Cyberpark Kozhikode.
  • SmartCity Kochi job openings — smartcity-kochi.in. Openings at SmartCity companies.
  • UL CyberPark jobs — ulcyberpark.com/jobs. Openings at UL CyberPark companies.
  • KINFRA careers — kinfra.org/careers. KINFRA recruitment notices.
  • Kerala State Job Portal — statejobportal.kerala.gov.in. Government of Kerala job portal.

Tech Keralam, policy and government

State and central government sites for IT policy, schemes and the new affiliation scheme. Read the current rules on the official page before applying.

  • Tech Keralam — techkeralam.in. The state’s common technology brand, launched on 8 October 2026.
  • IT Parks Affiliation Scheme — affiliation.techkeralam.in. Online registration for eligible IT firms, co-working spaces and private tech infrastructure outside government IT parks. Affiliation is not park tenancy and does not guarantee a subsidy.
  • Kerala IT Mission — itmission.kerala.gov.in. The state IT department’s technical arm.
  • Department of Industries & Commerce — keralaindustry.org. Kerala industries department.
  • Government of Kerala — kerala.gov.in. State government portal.
  • MeitY — meity.gov.in. Ministry of Electronics and IT, Government of India.
  • Digital India — digitalindia.gov.in. Central digital programmes.

Startup and founder support

Incubation, registration and founder programmes.

  • Kerala Startup Mission — startupmission.kerala.gov.in. Startup registration, incubation, grants and events in Kerala.
  • Startup India — startupindia.gov.in. DPIIT startup recognition and central schemes.
  • MeitY Startup Hub — msh.meity.gov.in. MeitY’s platform for tech startups and incubators.

Learning and certifications

Courses for freshers and working professionals. A certificate is not a job offer; check the price and what you receive before paying for anything.

  • Digital University Kerala — duk.ac.in. State digital technology university; runs the IndiaAI Centre of Excellence in Thiruvananthapuram.
  • ICT Academy of Kerala — ictkerala.org. IT skilling and training programmes.
  • SWAYAM — swayam.gov.in. Government of India online courses.
  • NPTEL — nptel.ac.in. IIT and IISc online courses with proctored exams.
  • Google Career Certificates — grow.google. Google’s paid certificate programmes.
  • Microsoft Learn — learn.microsoft.com. Free Microsoft training paths and certification prep.

Cyber safety and job-fraud help

Use these if a job offer asks for money, an OTP or remote access, or if your account is misused.

  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal — cybercrime.gov.in. Report online fraud. You can also call the helpline 1930.
  • Sanchar Saathi — sancharsaathi.gov.in. Report fraud calls and messages, and check mobile connections in your name.
  • CERT-In — cert-in.org.in. India’s computer emergency response team; security advisories.
