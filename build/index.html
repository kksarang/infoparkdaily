Government & central support

Startup schemes you can lean on

India's Startup India initiative and related central schemes give recognition, seed capital pathways, credit guarantees, and public-procurement access — strong tailwinds if you are ready to register and build.

  • Startup India / DPIIT Recognition, compliance ease, and nationwide ecosystem access for eligible ventures.
  • Seed Fund (SISFS) Early capital through approved incubators for proof-of-concept and product start.
  • Fund of Funds (FFS) Government-backed fund pathway that helps AIFs invest in Indian startups.
  • Credit Guarantee (CGSS) Loan guarantee cover expanded to help startups raise debt with less collateral friction.
  • GeM & public buyers Government e-Marketplace onboarding so startups can sell into public procurement.
  • State & Kerala IT Local incubators, Infopark/Technopark ecosystems, and state startup programmes alongside central schemes.

Scheme eligibility and benefits depend on official criteria. We help you prepare the product and plan — always verify latest rules on startupindia.gov.in.
