Getting started

What is Daily Web Studio?

A website builder from InfoparkDaily in Kochi. You set up a site by answering a few questions, and soon you’ll edit it visually and export it as clean code you own.

What can I do in Studio today?

Set up sites for 12 website types, choose a look, colour and pages, see a first look of your homepage, and save sites to your account. The template marketplace, visual editor and code export are next. See the roadmap for details.

How much does it cost?

Nothing during early access. Paid plans come later with a waitlist; done-for-you websites start at ₹1,999.

Which website type should I choose?

The closest match to what you do. If you’re not sure, choose Business website. Your type only decides the starting pages and suggested designs, and you can change anything later.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Setup is a few simple questions, and the visual editor will work by clicking and typing.

Account and saving

Where are my sites saved?

Signed in with a verified email, your sites save to your InfoparkDaily account and open on every device where you sign in. Signed out, they’re kept only in this browser.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Sites saved in your account are safe. Sites saved only in the browser (while signed out) are deleted. Sign in and use “Move to my account”, or download a backup from Settings first.

How do I move sites from this device to my account?

Sign in and verify your email. Studio shows a banner with a “Move to my account” button for any sites saved on the device.

Why do I need to verify my email?

Verification proves the email is yours before we store anything in your account. Google sign-ins are verified automatically.

How many sites can I have?

Up to 50 per account during early access.

Is it the same account as the resume builder?

Yes. One InfoparkDaily account works for Studio, the resume builder and saved jobs.

How do I back up my sites?

Open Settings in Studio and choose “Download backup (JSON)”.

Designs and templates

How many designs are there?

54 complete designs today, mostly portfolios plus business sites, stores, blogs and creator pages. More types arrive with the template marketplace.

Can I use a design in Studio now?

You can preview every design. They become editable Studio templates when the marketplace opens; until then our team can personalise any of them for you.

Are the people and businesses in the designs real?

No. All sample names, photos and details are fictional and replaced with yours.

Which website types have designs today?

Portfolios, business sites, online stores, blogs and creator pages. Restaurant, event, startup, corporate, real estate and landing page designs are coming.

Editing

When is the visual editor coming?

It’s being built now, right after the template marketplace. Early-access users get it first.

Will my current sites work in the editor?

Yes. Your type, look, colours and pages carry straight into it.

Can I edit on my phone?

Quick text and photo edits will work on a phone. Full design work is best on a laptop.

Stores and WhatsApp

Can I sell products?

Yes, simply: products with WhatsApp ordering and payment links work without a server. Full carts with stock tracking aren’t planned for Studio’s first version.

How do WhatsApp order buttons work?

Each product gets a button that opens WhatsApp with a message naming the product, so customers just tap send.

Do you take a commission?

No. Orders and payments go directly to you.

Can customers pay online?

Through payment links such as UPI or Razorpay that you share. Payment links inside Studio are planned.

Domains, hosting and export

Will I really own my site?

Yes. When code export launches you’ll download plain HTML, CSS and images with no Studio code inside, and you can keep them forever.

Where can I host my site for free?

Cloudflare Pages and Netlify allow business sites on their free plans. GitHub Pages is free for personal portfolios only.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Connect one you own, or ask our team to register one in your name through a done-for-you package.

Who owns the domain?

You do. Domains we register for you are registered in your name.

Do I get HTTPS?

Yes. Cloudflare Pages, Netlify and GitHub Pages add a secure certificate automatically.

Done-for-you and billing

What is done-for-you?

Our team personalises one of our designs with your content and launches it, from ₹1,999.

How long does a done-for-you site take?

It depends on the package and how quickly you share your content. We confirm the delivery estimate in your written quote.

How do revisions work?

The number of revision rounds is agreed in your written quote before you pay.

How do I pay safely?

Only pay after you receive a written quote from InfoparkDaily. We never ask for payment from unknown numbers.

Can I get a refund?

Studio Free has no charges to refund. For done-for-you work, cancellation and refund stages are agreed in your written quote before you pay.

Are taxes included in the price?

Any applicable taxes are shown in your written quote, so the total you agree to is the total you pay.

Privacy and security

What does Studio store about me?

Your account email and name, and for each site the setup answers: name, tagline, city, type, look, colour and pages. See the security page for details.

Who can see my sites?

Only you. Firebase security rules let each account read and change only its own sites.

How do I delete my sites?

Delete any site from My sites, or all of them from Settings.

How do I delete my account?

From your InfoparkDaily account page. Deleting your account also deletes all your Studio sites.
