Questions

Pricing questions

Something else on your mind? We usually reply within one working day.

Visit the help centre
Is Daily Web Studio really free?

Yes. Everything in Studio is free during early access, with no card needed. When paid plans launch, the Free plan stays, and exporting your own site will always be free.

When do Pro, Business and Agency launch?

After the template marketplace, visual editor and code export. Join a waitlist and we’ll message you first. Their features and prices are still being decided.

What do done-for-you prices include?

Each package lists what’s included. Domain eligibility, hosting, renewals, revisions, delivery time, taxes and cancellation terms are confirmed in a written quote before you pay.

Do prices include GST?

Any applicable taxes are shown in your written quote before you pay, so the total you agree to is the total you pay.

Can I get a refund?

Studio Free has no charges to refund. For done-for-you work, cancellation and refund stages are agreed in writing before payment.

Do you offer student or non-profit discounts?

We plan to. Mention it when you join a waitlist or order a done-for-you site, and we’ll tell you what we can offer.

How do I pay for done-for-you work?

We confirm the scope and total on WhatsApp or email first, then share payment details. Don’t pay anyone who contacts you without a written quote from InfoparkDaily.

Will my price go up later?

Early-access users will be told about any changes in advance. Sites you build stay yours and can be exported.
