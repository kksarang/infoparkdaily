Questions

Good to know.

What can I do in Studio today?

You can set up sites for 12 website types: choose a look, an accent colour and your pages, and see a first look of your homepage. The template marketplace comes next, followed by the visual editor and code export. Every site you set up now carries over.

Where are my sites saved?

During early access, sites are saved in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. You can download a backup from Settings. Syncing to your InfoparkDaily account arrives with the editor.

Do I need an account?

Not yet. Signing in is optional and uses the same InfoparkDaily account as our resume builder and job tools.

Will I really own the code?

Yes. Exported sites are plain HTML, CSS and images with no Studio code inside. You can host them anywhere, edit them in any editor, and keep them even if you stop using Studio.

Can I sell products on my site?

Yes, simply: product pages with WhatsApp ordering or payment links work without any server. Full stores with stock tracking and customer accounts need a real backend and aren’t part of Studio yet.

Is hosting really free?

Cloudflare Pages and Netlify have free plans that allow business sites. GitHub Pages is free too, but its terms don’t allow online shops or business sites, so use it for personal portfolios. You only pay if you choose a custom domain.
