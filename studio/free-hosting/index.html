Step-by-step

Publish on Cloudflare Pages in five minutes

Steps reflect each host’s free plan at the time of writing. Check the host’s own documentation for the latest details.

  1. Create a free Cloudflare account at dash.cloudflare.com.
  2. Open Workers & Pages, choose Create, then Pages, then Upload assets.
  3. Name your project (this becomes your-name.pages.dev) and upload your exported site folder.
  4. Click Deploy. Your site is live with HTTPS in about a minute.
  5. Optional: open Custom domains to connect a domain you own.

Visit the help centre
Is free hosting really free forever?

These hosts offer generous free plans that cover most small sites. Each host sets its own limits and may change them.

Which one should I choose?

Cloudflare Pages for business sites and stores, GitHub Pages for personal portfolios, Netlify if you want built-in forms.

What if my site gets lots of visitors?

Cloudflare Pages has unlimited bandwidth on its free plan. Netlify and GitHub Pages have soft monthly limits that small sites rarely reach.

Can you publish it for me?

Yes. Our done-for-you packages include hosting set-up.
