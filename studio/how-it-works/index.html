How it works

From idea to website, step by step.

Here’s the whole journey in Daily Web Studio, with an honest label on each step. Not ready to do it yourself? Our team can build it for you.

Start step 1 nowOr let us build it
1

Set up your site

Live

Answer five short questions: what you’re building, its name and one line about it, a look and accent colour, and the pages you need.

  • Pick from 12 website types
  • Add your name, tagline and city
  • Choose Clean, Bold, Warm or Editorial
  • Keep the suggested pages or add your own
  • See a first look of your homepage
Try it now
2

Choose a template

Next

Pick a full template made for your type. Your name, colours and pages carry over.

  • Filter templates by type and style
  • Preview each one on desktop and phone
  • Switch templates without losing your text
See the roadmap
3

Edit visually

Next

Click any text to change it, swap photos, reorder sections and set your fonts and colours.

  • Click-to-edit text and images
  • Sections list with drag to reorder
  • Desktop, tablet and phone views
  • Undo, redo and autosave
See the roadmap
4

Export and go live

Next

Download your site as clean code and publish it free, with your own domain if you like.

  • ZIP download of HTML, CSS and images
  • Free hosting guides for Cloudflare Pages, Netlify and GitHub Pages
  • Connect your own domain
