How much does a domain cost?

It depends on the ending. .in and .com are usually affordable for the first year; renewal prices can be higher, which is why we always show both.

I already have a domain. Can I use it?

Yes. You keep it with your current registrar and point it at your site.

Who owns the domain?

You do. Domains we register for you are registered in your name.

Can I get email on my domain?

Yes, through our Business Website + Domain + Mail package.
