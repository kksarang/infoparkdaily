Resume and career tools

Start here for a resume, then the official job pages for Infopark, Technopark, Cyberpark, SmartCity Kochi, UL CyberPark, and KINFRA. Most companies that use a park board appear on it. The board is not every company in the park. LinkedIn, Naukri, and Indeed have their own guides.

Free tools on this site

These run on InfoparkDaily. They do not log into an employer system, and they do not send your resume to a company.

  • Resume builder — type your details, preview, and download a PDF. Templates are free.
  • Resume templates — pick a layout, then edit it in the builder.
  • ATS checker — a readability and keyword check that stays on your device. It does not sign in to an employer’s applicant tracking system, and a high score is not an interview.
  • Jobs — openings we have copied from public portals, employer notices, and career pages. Confirm each one on the official link before you apply.
  • Resume writing guide and interview guide.

Infopark official jobs page

The Infopark jobs board is infopark.in/companies-job. Many companies that sit in Infopark Kochi post openings there. It is not a list of every Infopark company. A tenant can hire only on its own careers site, or only on LinkedIn or Naukri, and never appear on that board. When a company does use the board, open the company detail, read the last date, and apply the way that page says — email, form, or the company’s own careers link.

InfoparkDaily’s Infopark jobs page is our community copy of public listings. It is not the Infopark office and it is not complete. If the official page and our copy disagree, trust the official page.

Technopark

Technopark Trivandrum lists openings on technopark.in/job-search. The same limit applies: most companies that use the Technopark board appear there, not every company inside the campus. Our Technopark jobs page is a separate digest.

Cyberpark, Kozhikode

Kerala State IT Infrastructure Ltd publishes Cyberpark careers at cyberparks.in/careers. Read the post date. A listing can stay visible after the company has stopped hiring.

UL CyberPark

UL CyberPark, Kozhikode, uses a different website: ulcyberpark.com/jobs. Do not treat Cyberpark and UL CyberPark as one board. Check which park the company names, then open that park’s page.

SmartCity Kochi

SmartCity Kochi publishes openings at smartcity-kochi.in/media-hub/job-openings. The site is run by SmartCity (Kochi) Infrastructure Pvt Ltd. Their own pages say this brand is not the Government of India’s Smart Cities Mission and not Cochin Smart City Mission Limited. The openings list keeps older posts, so read the date on the article before you apply or travel. A 2023 post is not a current vacancy just because it is still on the page.

KINFRA

Kerala Industrial Infrastructure Development Corporation publishes staff notices at kinfra.org/careers. Some of those recruitments are taken through the Centre for Management Development at cmd.kerala.gov.in. Open the notification PDF on that site for the post, eligibility, dates, and the real apply link. This page does not copy vacancy numbers or pay.

Government recruitment sites

These are exam and employment boards. They are not the IT-park company boards above. Apply only through the notification on the official site. Do not pay an agent for a form the site itself provides.

  • Kerala PSC — Kerala state recruitments and exams.
  • UPSC — Union Public Service Commission examinations.
  • Staff Selection Commission — central government staff exams.
  • IBPS — bank recruitment exams run by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection.
  • National Career Service — the Government of India employment portal.

LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, and other job sites

Park boards do not carry every opening. Private sites do not either. Use them as search tools, then confirm the employer.

  • LinkedIn — create a profile, search jobs, write to HR, and publish a post.
  • Naukri — create the account, build the profile, and set alerts.
  • Indeed India — search, save an Indeed Resume, and tell Easy Apply from a company site.
  • Foundit, Internshala, and company career pages — when each one is useful.

A practical order

  1. Name the employer and the city. Kochi, Trivandrum, and Kozhikode are not interchangeable.
  2. Open the matching official board from this page, then the company’s own careers page if one is printed.
  3. Build one clear PDF in the resume builder. Change the top bullets for the role, not the whole design.
  4. Apply once, on the channel the posting names. Save the date, the role title, and the URL.
  5. One follow-up after a week is enough if the posting is still open. Do not send the same resume to five people in the same company on the same day.
