Resume builder

  1. Is the resume builder really free?

    Yes. Every resume template, the live preview and PDF export are ₹0, and so is the ATS checker. Portfolio websites are a separate, paid service.

  2. Do I need an account?

    You can browse every template without one. Sign in to edit and save — drafts stay in the cloud so you can continue anytime.

  3. Will applicant tracking systems read my PDF?

    The PDF keeps real, selectable text and clear section headings. Run the ATS checker for a second pass — it is a guide, not a guarantee from any employer’s tracker.

ATS checker

  1. Is the ATS resume checker free?

    Yes. Upload a PDF or DOCX, or paste text, and get a readability and structure report without paying.

  2. Does my resume get uploaded to your servers?

    No. Parsing runs on your device. InfoparkDaily never receives the file or the score.

  3. What does an ATS-friendly resume mean?

    Real selectable text, clear headings like Experience and Education, and a simple layout so trackers can read your details.

  4. Can I check a resume against a job description?

    Yes — paste the posting for keyword overlap suggestions, then fix phrasing in the free Resume Builder if needed.

  5. Does a high ATS score mean I will get an interview?

    No. The score is a readability and structure guide only. Employers still decide who to shortlist.

Job email samples

  1. How do I write an email when sending my resume?

    Clear subject with the job title, one short intro, attach Resume_YourName.pdf, and mail only the official address.

  2. What subject line works?

    Include your name and the exact title, for example: Application — Frontend Developer — Your Name.

  3. Attach a resume or send a link?

    Most Indian posts expect a PDF attached. Prefer selectable text — build one in the Resume Builder and check it with the ATS Checker.

  4. How long before a follow-up?

    About 7–10 days if the posting is still open. One polite note is enough.
