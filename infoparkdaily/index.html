Employer FAQ

Questions from hiring teams.

Is posting free?

Yes. Both the quick hiring request and the employer workspace are free. Every post is reviewed before it goes live.

How long does review take?

Usually within one working day. You can track a quick request with its ID, and employer posts show their status in your dashboard.

Can I post a walk-in drive?

Yes. Choose Walk-in drive and add the date, time, venue, map link and the documents candidates should bring.

Do I need an account?

No for a one-time post — use a quick hiring request. Create a free employer account to manage multiple posts, applications and hires.

What gets rejected?

Posts that ask candidates for money, use unverifiable contacts, or have unclear company details. We’ll add a note so you can fix and resubmit.

Where do approved posts appear?

On the InfoparkDaily jobs board, its park pages and job search — and they can be shared to our WhatsApp and Instagram job channels.
