THE STEPS

Five steps, one clear status.

You always know where your post stands.

  1. 01

    Submit

    Fill one guided form — a regular job, a walk-in drive or a quick job alert. No account needed.

  2. 02

    We verify

    The InfoparkDaily team checks the company, contacts and details — usually within one working day.

  3. 03

    Track it

    Use your request ID (or your dashboard) to see Submitted → Verified → Live, plus any note from us.

  4. 04

    Go live

    Approved posts appear on the jobs board and park pages, and can be shared to our WhatsApp & Instagram alerts.

  5. 05

    Hire

    Candidates apply the way you chose — by email, your careers link, or by walking in.

FAQ

Questions from hiring teams.

Can’t find an answer? Contact us

Is posting free?

Yes. Both posting without sign-in and the employer workspace are free. Every post is reviewed before it goes live.

How long does review take?

Usually within one working day. Track a no-sign-in post with its request ID; employer posts show their status in your dashboard.

Do I need an account?

No. Use Post a job for any job, walk-in or job alert. Create a free employer account only if you want a dashboard for all your posts.

What if my post needs changes?

We add a short note explaining what to fix. You'll see it on the tracking page or in your dashboard, and you can submit an updated post.

Where do approved posts appear?

On the InfoparkDaily jobs board, its park pages and job search — and they can be shared to our WhatsApp and Instagram job channels.

I'm looking for a job. Is this for me?

This hub is for hiring teams. Job seekers can browse the jobs board and set up free job alerts on WhatsApp or email.

How do I report a fake or misleading post?

Contact us with the job link and a short description. Never send passwords or verification codes to anyone.
