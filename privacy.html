Privacy Policy

InfoparkDaily respects your privacy. We run a static community website and social channels (@infoparkdaily, @infoparkdaily.jobs, @infoparkdaily.media, WhatsApp Channel / Group).

  • What we may collect: information you voluntarily send via contact forms, email, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, or business collaboration requests (name, contact details, message content).
  • How we use it: only to reply to your inquiry, share relevant community updates you asked for, or handle correction / removal / collaboration requests.
  • What we do not do: we do not sell personal data. We do not charge candidates for jobs. We do not claim to be official Infopark or company recruiters.
  • Third-party sites: job apply links open official company careers pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Always review them before submitting personal data.
  • Cookies / analytics: the website may use basic browser storage for theme preference and service-worker caching for performance. We do not use this to sell ads against your identity.
  • Your choices: you may request correction or removal of content you believe is inaccurate or wrongly attributed by contacting us through official channels listed on this site.

How to stay safe

  • Apply only on the company’s official careers portal or verified HR email.
  • Never pay money, OTP, or “registration fees” for a job or internship shared by InfoparkDaily.
  • Ignore anyone claiming to be InfoparkDaily staff asking for payment — report them.
  • Double-check company name, location, and posting date before travelling for walk-ins.
  • Follow our official handles only: Instagram @infoparkdaily / @infoparkdaily.jobs and our listed WhatsApp Channel.
