Roadmap

What you’ll learn

Follow the modules in order. Finish a small project at the end of each one.

  1. 01

    Principles

    2 weeks
    • Hierarchy, balance, contrast
    • Typography
    • Colour theory
    • Grids
  2. 02

    Tools

    4 weeks
    • Photoshop: layers, masks, retouching
    • Illustrator: vectors and logos
    • InDesign basics
    • Canva for quick work
  3. 03

    Branding & print

    2 weeks
    • Logos and brand guides
    • Print: CMYK, bleed, 300 DPI
    • Packaging basics
    • Mock-ups
  4. 04

    Portfolio

    2 weeks
    • Behance/Dribbble profile
    • Case studies
    • Client briefs
    • Feedback handling
