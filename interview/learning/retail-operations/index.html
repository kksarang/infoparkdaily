Roadmap

What you’ll learn

Follow the modules in order. Finish a small project at the end of each one.

  1. 01

    Customer service & selling

    2 weeks
    • Greeting and needs
    • Upselling and cross-selling
    • Handling returns
    • Complaints
  2. 02

    Store operations

    1 week
    • POS and billing
    • Cash handling
    • Opening/closing
    • Safety
  3. 03

    Inventory

    1 week
    • Stock counting
    • FIFO/FEFO
    • Shrinkage control
    • Replenishment
  4. 04

    Merchandising & KPIs

    1 week
    • Visual merchandising
    • Footfall and conversion
    • Average bill value
    • Daily reports
