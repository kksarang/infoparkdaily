Anatomy of a professional email

Subject

Role + your name (+ experience). Use the post’s format if given.

Greeting

“Dear Ms. Priya,” if a name is known; otherwise “Dear Hiring Team,”.

Opening

Which role, where you saw it, who you are.

Proof

2–3 lines on matching skills or results.

Close

Availability, thanks, attachment mention.

Signature

Name, phone, LinkedIn. No images.

01

Job application email

Applying for a job and attaching your resume.

When to send this email

  • The job post asks you to apply by email
  • Applying to a walk-in or park listing with an HR email
  • Within a few days of the post going live

Email subject

Application – Junior QA Engineer – Anjali Menon (Fresher, 2026)

Sample email

Replace the names, role and details with your own

Subject: Application – Junior QA Engineer – Anjali Menon (Fresher, 2026)

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Junior QA Engineer position at Brightloop Technologies, which I found on InfoparkDaily.

I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science (2026) from ABC College of Engineering. In my final-year project I wrote and executed 120 test cases for a delivery-tracking app, logged defects in Jira and used SQL to verify data.

I am available to join immediately and can work from Infopark, Kochi. My resume is attached for your review.

Thank you for your time.

Regards,
Anjali Menon
+91 98XXX XXXXX
linkedin.com/in/anjalimenon

Do

  • Use the exact role title (and job ID) from the post
  • Say where you saw the job
  • Keep the body to 4–6 short lines
  • Attach one PDF named Firstname_Lastname_Role_Resume.pdf

Don’t

  • Send an empty email with only an attachment
  • CC many companies in one email
  • Write a long life story
  • Use an unprofessional email address
02

Sending your resume to HR

Sending your resume directly to a recruiter, with or without a posted opening.

When to send this email

  • You have an HR or careers email but no specific listing
  • A contact told you the team is hiring
  • Following up a phone call from a recruiter who asked for your CV

Email subject

Resume – QA Engineer – 2 Yrs Experience – Nikhil Joseph

Sample email

Replace the names, role and details with your own

Subject: Resume – QA Engineer – 2 Yrs Experience – Nikhil Joseph

Dear Ms. Priya,

As discussed on our call today, please find my resume attached for QA Engineer roles at Brightloop.

I have 2 years of experience in manual and API testing (Postman, Jira, SQL) for e-commerce applications, and I am currently learning Selenium with Java.

Current CTC: ₹3.6 LPA · Notice period: 30 days · Location: Kochi

Please let me know if you need any other details.

Regards,
Nikhil Joseph
+91 94XXX XXXXX

Do

  • Mention the call or referral that led to this email
  • Add CTC and notice only if the recruiter asked
  • Send within a few hours of the call

Don’t

  • Send the same resume to every address you find
  • Attach certificates and photos unless asked
  • Share a Drive link without access set to view
03

Follow-up email

Following up after submitting a job application.

When to send this email

  • 7–10 working days after applying with no response
  • After the date HR promised an update
  • Once more about a week later at most

Email subject

Follow-up: Application for Junior QA Engineer – Anjali Menon

Sample email

Replace the names, role and details with your own

Subject: Follow-up: Application for Junior QA Engineer – Anjali Menon

Dear Hiring Team,

I applied for the Junior QA Engineer role on 4 November and wanted to check whether there is an update on my application.

I remain very interested in the role and am available for a test or interview at your convenience. I have attached my resume again for easy reference.

Thank you,
Anjali Menon
+91 98XXX XXXXX

Do

  • Reply in the same email thread if possible
  • Mention the role and the date you applied
  • Keep it short and polite

Don’t

  • Follow up daily
  • Sound demanding (“Why haven’t you replied?”)
  • Follow up more than twice
04

Interview confirmation email

Confirming an interview invitation professionally.

When to send this email

  • Within a few hours of receiving the invite
  • When you need to confirm a slot, mode or venue
  • When you need to request a different time

Email subject

Re: Interview Invitation – Junior QA Engineer

Sample email

Replace the names, role and details with your own

Subject: Re: Interview Invitation – Junior QA Engineer

Dear Meera,

Thank you for the invitation. I confirm my availability for the technical interview on Thursday, 13 November at 11:00 am (Google Meet).

Please let me know if I should prepare anything specific or bring any documents.

Regards,
Anjali Menon
+91 98XXX XXXXX

Do

  • Repeat the date, time, mode and time zone to avoid confusion
  • Reply to all if the invite came to multiple people
  • Save the meeting link and test it beforehand

Don’t

  • Ignore the invite until the last minute
  • Ask to reschedule without offering alternatives
  • Change the subject line
05

Thank-you email

Thanking the interviewer after an interview.

When to send this email

  • The same day, within a few hours of the interview
  • After any round — HR, technical or managerial
  • When you have the interviewer’s or recruiter’s email

Email subject

Thank you – Technical Interview for Java Developer – Rahul Nair

Sample email

Replace the names, role and details with your own

Subject: Thank you – Technical Interview for Java Developer – Rahul Nair

Dear Mr. Varghese,

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today about the Java Developer role. I enjoyed discussing the payment reconciliation service and how your team handles retries.

Our conversation made me even more excited about the opportunity to contribute to your team. Please let me know if you need any further information from me.

Best regards,
Rahul Nair
+91 97XXX XXXXX

Do

  • Mention one specific topic from the conversation
  • Keep it under 120 words
  • Send separate notes if you met different people

Don’t

  • Re-argue an answer you got wrong
  • Ask “Did I get selected?”
  • Copy-paste a generic message

More ready-to-use templates

Asking for a referral

Hi Arun,

Hope you're doing well. Northwind Labs is hiring a Data Analyst (Job ID 4512): <job link>

I have 2 years of experience in SQL, Power BI and Python reporting and think I match the role well. Would you be comfortable referring me? My resume and a 3-line summary are below.

• 2 years as Data Analyst at ABC Corp – 12 Power BI dashboards for sales leadership
• SQL (joins, window functions), Python (pandas)
• Notice period: 30 days

Thank you either way!
Sneha

Requesting to reschedule

Subject: Request to Reschedule – Technical Interview – Rahul Nair

Dear Meera,

Thank you for scheduling my interview on 13 November at 11 am. Unfortunately, I have a commitment I can't move that morning. Would any of these slots work?

• 13 Nov, after 4 pm
• 14 Nov, any time
• 15 Nov, before 1 pm

I apologise for the inconvenience.

Regards,
Rahul Nair

Accepting an offer

Subject: Acceptance of Offer – Java Developer – Rahul Nair

Dear Meera,

Thank you for the offer for the Java Developer position. I am pleased to accept it on the terms stated in the offer letter dated 10 November, with a joining date of 15 December at Infopark, Kochi. I have attached the signed copy.

Please let me know the documents required before joining.

Warm regards,
Rahul Nair

Declining an offer politely

Subject: Offer for Java Developer – Rahul Nair

Dear Meera,

Thank you very much for the offer and for your time throughout the process. After careful thought, I have decided to accept another opportunity that aligns more closely with my current goals.

I truly appreciated meeting the team and hope our paths cross again.

Best regards,
Rahul Nair

Asking for feedback after rejection

Subject: Thank you – Junior QA Engineer interview – Anjali Menon

Dear Meera,

Thank you for letting me know about the decision. If possible, could you share one or two areas I could improve? It would help me prepare better. I'd be glad to be considered for similar roles in future.

Thank you again,
Anjali Menon

Resignation email

Subject: Resignation – Rahul Nair – Java Developer

Dear Arjun,

Please accept this email as formal notice of my resignation from the role of Java Developer. As per my 30-day notice period, my last working day will be 14 December 2026.

Thank you for the guidance over the past three years. I'll make sure the handover is complete and documented.

Regards,
Rahul Nair

Watch out

Write only to addresses printed on the official notice or company website. Never send OTPs, bank details or Aadhaar scans by email to an unverified recruiter.
