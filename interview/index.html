🎓 Fresher / student plan

No experience yet? That’s fine. Follow these steps in order — most freshers get interview-ready in 2–4 weeks.

  • 8steps
  • 2–4weeks
  • Freeno sign-up
  1. Step 1

    Make your first resume

    Use a simple one-page template. Put projects, internships and skills first — you don’t need work experience.

    30 min Build my resume →
  2. Step 2

    Check it against a job

    Paste a job description and see which keywords are missing. Fix them honestly.

    10 min Check my resume →
  3. Step 3

    Find fresher jobs & walk-ins

    Look for Trainee, Associate, Junior and Intern roles. Walk-ins are great practice.

    15 min a day See fresher jobs →
  4. Step 4

    Send a proper application email

    Short subject, 5–6 lines, resume attached as PDF. Copy a template and change the details.

    10 min Get email template →
  5. Step 5

    Practise HR & aptitude questions

    Start with “Tell me about yourself”, then aptitude and basics of your subject.

    30 min a day Practise fresher questions →
  6. Step 6

    Learn & try live code

    Fill skill gaps with free courses and practise code right in your browser.

    Ongoing Start learning →
  7. Step 7

    Prepare for each interview round

    Understand HR, test, technical and manager rounds — what they check and how to answer.

    2–3 hrs Open the guide →
  8. Step 8

    Got an offer? Check it first

    Understand CTC vs in-hand, bond, joining date and documents before you accept.

    20 min Read offer tips →

💼 Experienced professional plan

Switching jobs? Work through these steps while you’re still employed — start 2–3 months before you want to move.

  • 7steps
  • 4–8weeks
  • Freeno sign-up
  1. Step 1

    Update your resume with results

    Show achievements with numbers, not duties. Keep titles and dates exactly as in your letters.

    45 min Update my resume →
  2. Step 2

    Match it to the roles you want

    Check keywords against 2–3 target job descriptions.

    10 min per job Check my resume →
  3. Step 3

    Find experienced roles & referrals

    Use InfoparkDaily’s experienced and referral filters, plus LinkedIn and Naukri.

    15 min a day See experienced jobs →
  4. Step 4

    Reach recruiters and referrers

    Send short referral requests and recruiter replies that get answered.

    15 min Get email templates →
  5. Step 5

    Practise technical & managerial questions

    System questions, project stories and STAR answers for manager rounds.

    30 min a day Practise experienced questions →
  6. Step 6

    Prepare salary & notice-period answers

    Know your current CTC, expected range, notice period and buyout options.

    30 min Salary & negotiation guide →
  7. Step 7

    Compare offers, then resign right

    Check the offer letter, accept in writing, then resign and hand over cleanly.

    30 min Offer & resignation guide →

Help

Not sure where to start?

I’m a fresher with no experience. Is this for me?

Yes. Choose Fresher / student above. Every step is written for people applying for their first job — projects and internships count.

I don’t know which job to apply for.

Start with fresher jobs or walk-ins, read a few job descriptions, and pick roles that match your degree and projects. The Learning page shows what each role needs.

I have an interview tomorrow. What should I do?

Practise “Tell me about yourself” and the HR questions, revise your resume line by line, and read the HR call and technical interview chapters.

Is everything free? Do I need to sign up?

Everything here is free and works without signing up. Your progress is saved in your browser. Sign in only if you want to save resumes in the Resume Builder.

Is InfoparkDaily the company hiring me?

No. InfoparkDaily lists openings from companies and public sources. Always apply through the method on the job listing, and never pay anyone for a job.
