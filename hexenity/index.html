09 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about our execution model and how Hexenity supports business growth.

What kind of businesses does Hexenity work with?

We partner with startups, agencies, non-technical businesses, and enterprise teams that need digital systems, applications, automation, branding, or growth infrastructure.

Do we need an internal tech team before starting?

No. Hexenity can act as your external innovation and execution team — handling discovery, design, engineering, launch, and optimization with clear governance.

Can Hexenity handle both technology and growth services?

Yes. We support product engineering plus digital growth capabilities including branding, SEO, campaign systems, and conversion-focused optimization.

How do you keep projects professional and predictable?

Every engagement follows milestone planning, sprint reviews, QA checkpoints, architecture governance, and transparent status reporting.

Can you modernize an existing product or broken project?

Absolutely. We take over existing codebases, improve architecture and UX, fix performance issues, and move products toward scalable, maintainable standards.

Do you provide post-launch support and growth planning?

Yes. We provide ongoing support, analytics review, performance optimization, and strategic roadmap guidance after launch.
