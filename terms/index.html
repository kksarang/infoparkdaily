The Recruit page — and the matching job request flow in the InfoparkDaily mobile app — lets companies, HR, or authorised contacts ask InfoparkDaily to consider sharing an opening. This exists to help job seekers see real opportunities — not to guarantee placement, interviews, or offers. Job requests shown in the app follow the same review rules as the website.
