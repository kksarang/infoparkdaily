5. Recruit — company hiring requests

The Recruit page lets companies, HR, or authorised contacts ask InfoparkDaily to consider sharing an opening. This exists to help job seekers see real opportunities — not to guarantee placement, interviews, or offers.

  • Submitting a Recruit form is a request only. It does not automatically create a Jobs listing.
  • We review and attempt to verify company identity, role details, and contacts. An opening is added to the Jobs page only if we accept it after that review.
  • If a request looks fake, incomplete, misleading, unauthorised, or cannot be reasonably verified, we will not add it to Jobs. We may ask for official confirmation or decline without listing.
  • By submitting Recruit (or a similar hiring request via Contact / email), you confirm the information is truthful and that you are authorised to share it. Providing false hiring information may lead to rejection, removal, and reporting where appropriate.
  • Even after we list an opening, candidates must still verify on official company channels before applying, travelling, or sharing documents. Our verification is a community safety step — not a legal or employer guarantee.
  • We may update, pause, or remove Recruit-sourced listings at any time. Further privacy handling of Recruit form data is described in the Privacy Policy.
