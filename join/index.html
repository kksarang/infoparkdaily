Safety, listings & terms — please read

We upload 100+ job openings every day to our Instagram channel, WhatsApp job channel, WhatsApp community group, and infoparkdaily.online/jobs. Most posts come from employers, HR teams and job seekers who share with us — we add notes when we can, but we are not the hiring company and we do not run interviews.

Verify before you apply. Details can change or expire. We cannot promise that every listing is still open or genuine. Always confirm on the company’s official website, careers email or Infopark/Technopark/Cyberpark portal before you share documents, attend a walk-in, or travel for an interview.

Never pay anyone for a job. Do not pay for an application, registration, training, “security deposit”, interview slot, or offer letter. Legitimate employers do not ask for money through personal UPI or unknown accounts. If someone claims to be InfoparkDaily and demands payment, it is a scam — we only share information; we never collect fees for placements.
