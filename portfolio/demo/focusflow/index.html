THE PROBLEM

Too many apps. Too little done.

Planning tools have become projects of their own. You spend the morning organising instead of working.

FocusFlow does one thing: it shows today’s three most important tasks and starts a focus timer.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, every morning.

  1. 01

    Pick three

    Choose today’s three tasks.

  2. 02

    Press start

    A 25-minute focus timer begins.

  3. 03

    Close the day

    One tap moves the rest to tomorrow.
