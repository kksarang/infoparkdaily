BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Good questions. Clear answers.

What is included in the ₹999 Portfolio Website plan?＋

A personalised one-page website based on any listed design, responsive layouts, your professional content, approved resume/contact links, and basic SEO. A new domain is excluded — use our subdomain or connect one you own.

What is included in the ₹1,999 Portfolio + Domain plan?＋

The portfolio website plus one eligible standard-price custom domain for the first year, subject to availability and the registration allowance confirmed in your quote.

What is the ₹4,999 Extra Pages plan?＋

A multi-page portfolio with separate About, Services and Contact pages, plus an eligible domain. Ideal when one scrolling page is not enough.

What is Shop Portfolio + Domain?＋

A portfolio with a simple product or catalogue section and WhatsApp/enquiry order links — no payment gateway. Includes an eligible domain for the first year.

What about business email and custom business sites?＋

Business Website + Domain + Mail is quoted separately through Enitexa.ai — including professional email setup, custom pages and integrations.

Can I choose any listed template?＋

Yes. Every listed template has a complete demo. Choose one design and our team personalises it with your content.

Is the domain included forever?＋

No. Only the first year of an eligible registration is included in domain plans. Renewals are disclosed before you order.

Which domains are eligible?＋

Eligibility depends on availability, extension, and registration cost. The covered cost limit and qualifying extensions must be confirmed in your written quote.

Can I use a domain I already own?＋

Yes, enquire about connecting a supported existing domain with the ₹999 plan. We confirm registrar access and hosting compatibility before work starts.

Is hosting included?＋

Hosting provider, included period, HTTPS support, and costs must be confirmed in your quote. We do not promise free or lifetime hosting.

What are the renewal charges?＋

We disclose the domain and hosting renewal estimates separately before payment, along with the renewal manager and expiry dates.

How long will my website take?＋

We confirm a delivery estimate after reviewing your content and scope. The timeline depends on complete materials, revisions, and domain setup.

What content do I need to provide?＋

Your name, headline, biography, skills, education, real project details, experience if applicable, images, resume PDF, and approved public contact links.

Can I use the service as a fresher?＋

Yes. Fresh Start and Campus Portfolio put education, academic projects, and internships first. We remove sections that do not apply.

Can I change colours and images?＋

Yes. Name, copy, profile image, accent colour, projects, skills, and contact links can be personalised within the selected layout.

How many revisions are included?＋

Two consolidated rounds are the proposed allowance. The exact revision count is confirmed in your written quote before payment.

Can I request a completely custom design?＋

Yes, as a separate quote through Enitexa.ai. Template personalisation does not include a full redesign or custom applications.

Can I update the website later?＋

Later changes can be arranged at an agreed cost. A CMS, editing dashboard, and self-service editor are not included.

Will the website work on phones?＋

The listed demos include responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts. You can inspect them using the preview device controls.

Can I connect LinkedIn, GitHub, Behance, and my resume?＋

Yes. We use your approved profile links and final resume PDF. You approve which email and phone appear publicly.

Who owns the domain?＋

The customer must be recorded as the domain owner. Registrar access, renewal responsibility, and transfer steps are agreed before purchase.

What happens if I do not renew?＋

Domain or hosting expiry can make your website unavailable. Grace periods and recovery fees depend on the provider.

What are the cancellation and refund terms?＋

Cancellation stages, refundable amounts, and non-refundable third-party costs must be agreed in writing before payment.

Are taxes included?＋

Tax treatment and the final payable total are confirmed in the written quote. Do not pay until the total and scope are agreed.
