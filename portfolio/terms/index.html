BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Good questions. Clear answers.

What is included in ₹2,999?＋

A personalised one-page website based on any listed design, responsive layouts, your professional content, approved resume/contact links, and basic SEO. A new domain is excluded. Final project limits and hosting terms are confirmed in writing.

What is included in ₹3,999?＋

The portfolio website plus one eligible standard-price custom domain for the first year, subject to availability and the registration allowance confirmed in your quote.

Can I choose any listed template?＋

Yes. Every listed template has a complete demo. Choose one design and our team personalises it with your content.

Is the domain included forever?＋

No. Only the first year of an eligible registration is included in the domain package. Domain and hosting renewals are disclosed before you order.

Which domains are eligible?＋

Eligibility depends on availability, extension, and registration cost. The covered cost limit and qualifying extensions must be confirmed in your written quote. Premium names and higher-priced extensions need a separate quote.

Can I use a domain I already own?＋

Yes, enquire about connecting a supported existing domain with the ₹2,999 package. We confirm registrar access and hosting compatibility before work starts.

Is hosting included?＋

Hosting provider, included period, HTTPS support, and costs must be confirmed in your quote. We do not promise free or lifetime hosting.

What are the renewal charges?＋

We disclose the domain and hosting renewal estimates separately before payment, along with the renewal manager and expiry dates. Future provider prices may change.

How long will my website take?＋

We confirm a delivery estimate after reviewing your content and scope. The timeline depends on complete materials, revisions, and domain setup.

What content do I need to provide?＋

Your name, headline, biography, skills, education, real project details, experience if applicable, images, resume PDF, and approved public contact links. A checklist is provided after enquiry.

Can I use the service as a fresher?＋

Yes. Fresh Start and Campus Portfolio put education, academic projects, and internships first. We remove sections that do not apply.

Can I change colours and images?＋

Yes. Name, copy, profile image, accent colour, projects, skills, and contact links can be personalised within the selected layout.

How many revisions are included?＋

Two consolidated rounds are the proposed allowance. The exact revision count and what constitutes a round are confirmed in your written quote before payment.

Can I request a completely custom design?＋

Yes, as a separate quote. Template personalisation does not include a full redesign, custom applications, or unlimited new sections.

Can I update the website later?＋

Later changes can be arranged at an agreed cost. A CMS, editing dashboard, unlimited updates, and self-service editor are not included.

Can I add more projects later?＋

Yes, subject to the agreed layout and a change quote. Up to six project entries are the proposed starting scope, confirmed before ordering.

Will the website work on phones?＋

The listed demos include responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts. You can inspect them using the preview device controls.

Can I connect LinkedIn, GitHub, Behance, and my resume?＋

Yes. We use your approved profile links and final resume PDF. You explicitly approve which email address and phone number can appear publicly.

Is business email included?＋

No. Mailboxes and paid email services require a separate arrangement. Your existing email can be linked.

Who owns the domain?＋

The customer must be recorded as the domain owner. Registrar access, management, renewal responsibility, and transfer steps are agreed before purchase.

Can I move the website elsewhere?＋

Handover files, repository access, licences, hosting portability, and any migration work are recorded in the service agreement.

What happens if I do not renew?＋

Domain or hosting expiry can make your website unavailable. Any grace periods, retention, or restoration fees depend on the provider and are explained when choosing the service.

What are the cancellation and refund terms?＋

Cancellation stages, refundable amounts, and non-refundable third-party costs must be agreed in writing before payment. This enquiry does not place a paid order.

Are taxes included?＋

Tax treatment and the final payable total are confirmed in the written quote. Do not make payment until the total and scope are agreed.
