Onam belongs to every Malayali household — Tech Onam 2K26 brings that same warmth to Kerala’s biggest IT address.

Infopark Kochi is home to a large share of Kerala’s tech workforce. Tech Onam 2K26 is a community-led celebration by InfoparkMomentz and InfoparkDaily — pookalam, Onasadya, games, culture, and a festival day built for techies at Infopark Kakkanad.

Full programme details will be shared once confirmed. We are open to sponsors and collaborators who want to own a piece of Kerala’s most loved tech-campus Onam.
