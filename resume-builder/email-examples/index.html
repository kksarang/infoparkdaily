    Job application email sample

    Any posted role when the notice says apply by email. Works for office, sales, campus, and tech jobs.

    Apply
    Subject 
    Application — [Job Title] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Manager / Hiring Team],

I am applying for the [Job Title] position at [Company], as listed on [job board / company careers page / LinkedIn]. I am based in [City] and have [fresher / N years] of experience in [field].

I bring [skill or result 1] and [skill or result 2].

Phone: [phone]
Email: [email]

Thank you for your time. I am happy to join a call at your convenience.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: Use the exact job title from the posting.

    Job application email for freshers

    Recent graduate or first full-time role. Lead with education or projects — not a long story.

    Fresher
    Subject 
    [Your Name] — Application for [Job Title] (Fresher)
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Team],

I am a fresher applying for [Job Title] at [Company] (posted on [source]). I completed [degree, year] and I am based in [City].

Relevant preparation: [project / internship / coursework]; [tool or skill from the job post].

Phone: [phone]
Email: [email]

Thank you for considering my application.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Mistake to avoid: Claiming years of experience you do not have.

    Experienced hire

    Clear years, domain, and notice period when the post asks for immediate joiners.

    Experienced
    Subject 
    Application — [Job Title] — [N]+ years — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Recruiter name / Hiring Team],

Please consider my application for [Job Title] at [Company], listed on [source]. I have [N] years of experience in [domain / function], currently based in [City].

Recent work: [result or responsibility 1]; [result or responsibility 2]. Notice period: [immediate / N days].

Phone: [phone]
Email: [email]

Happy to discuss further.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: Put notice period early if the ad says “immediate joiners”.

    Internship application

    For intern or trainee posts. Mention available dates clearly.

    Internship
    Subject 
    Internship application — [Role / Team] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Team],

I am applying for the [Internship title] at [Company], as listed on [source]. I am a [year / course] student at [college] and can join from [start month].

I have worked on [project or course] using [tools]. Available [full-time / part-time] during [period].

Phone: [phone]
Email: [email]

Thank you.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: State when you can start — dates get filtered first.

    Walk-in confirmation

    When the notice asks you to email before attending, or to confirm date and venue.

    Walk-in
    Subject 
    Walk-in confirmation — [Job Title] — [Date] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Team],

I would like to attend the walk-in for [Job Title] at [Company] on [date] at [venue / building if printed], as listed on [source].

I am a [fresher / N years] candidate based in [City]. Please confirm if I should report at a specific time.

Phone: [phone]
Email: [email]

Thank you.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Safety: Confirm venue on the official notice the morning of the drive.

    Email asking for a job referral

    Someone shared an HR email. Still verify the domain when you can.

    Referral
    Subject 
    Application — [Job Title] — referred by [Referrer Name] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Recruiter name],

[Referrer Name] suggested I write about the [Job Title] opening at [Company]. I am based in [City] with [fresher / N years] experience in [field].

Brief fit: [proof line 1]; [proof line 2].

Phone: [phone]
Email: [email]

Thank you for your time.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Mistake: Name-dropping without a real referral.

    Follow-up email after applying for a job

    One polite check-in if the posting is still open. Do not mail daily.

    Follow-up
    Subject 
    Follow-up — Application — [Job Title] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Team / Recruiter name],

I applied for [Job Title] at [Company] on [apply date]. The role still appears open on [source], so I am writing once to check if you need any further information.

Thank you for your consideration.

Phone: [phone]
Email: [email]

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Mistake: Sounding frustrated in the first line.

    Thank you email after interview

    Same day or next working day after a call, video, or in-person round.

    Thank you
    Subject 
    Thank you — [Job Title] interview — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Interviewer / Recruiter name],

Thank you for speaking with me about the [Job Title] role at [Company]. I appreciated the discussion on [topic you discussed].

I remain interested in the opportunity. Please let me know if you need anything else from my side.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[phone] · [email]

    Tip: Keep salary talks out of this note unless they asked.

    Cold email for job opportunity

    When you admire a company but there is no public vacancy. Keep it humble and short.

    Cold outreach
    Subject 
    Interest in [Team / Function] roles — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Team / Talent team],

I am a [fresher / N years] [role type] based in [City]. I am interested in future openings on your [team / function] and wanted to introduce myself.

Background: [one proof line]. I am open to [full-time / contract] roles and happy to reconnect when a matching role opens.

Phone: [phone]
Email: [email]

Thank you for your time.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: Prefer careers@ or talent@ on the company site — not random personal inboxes.

    How to withdraw a job application (email)

    You accepted another offer or cannot continue. Stay polite and brief.

    Withdraw
    Subject 
    Withdrawing application — [Job Title] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Team / Recruiter name],

Thank you for considering my application for [Job Title] at [Company]. I need to withdraw my application at this time [optional: due to another commitment].

I appreciate your time and wish the team well with the hire.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Mistake: Ghosting after a scheduled interview.

    Career change / different field

    Moving into a new function. Connect transferable skills to the job post in two lines.

    Apply
    Subject 
    Application — [Job Title] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Team],

I am applying for [Job Title] at [Company] (seen on [source]). I have [N] years in [previous field] and I am transitioning into [new field], based in [City].

Transferable fit: [skill that matches the JD]; [project, course, or result].

Phone: [phone]
Email: [email]

Thank you for your consideration.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: Do not hide the career change — mismatched titles get noticed anyway.

    Remote / work-from-home role

    State city or time zone and meeting availability so HR knows you can join calls.

    Apply
    Subject 
    Application — [Job Title] (Remote) — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Team],

I am applying for the remote [Job Title] role at [Company], listed on [source]. I work from [City / time zone] and am available for [IST / overlap hours] meetings.

Fit: [proof line 1]; [proof line 2]. Notice period: [immediate / N days].

Phone: [phone]
Email: [email]

Thank you.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: Only claim remote if the posting allows it.

    Reply to a recruiter who contacted you

    A recruiter messaged you on LinkedIn, Naukri or email about a role. Reply fast, confirm interest and share the basics they will ask anyway.

    Apply
    Subject 
    Re: [Job Title] at [Company] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Recruiter Name],

Thank you for reaching out about the [Job Title] role at [Company]. I am interested and would like to know more.

Quick details for your screening:
- Current role: [current title] at [current company]
- Experience: [N] years in [field]
- Current location: [City] · open to [office / hybrid / remote]
- Notice period: [immediate / N days]
- Current CTC: [amount] · Expected CTC: [amount or "open to discuss"]

My resume is attached. I am available for a call on [day] between [time] and [time].

Regards,
[Your Name]
[phone]

    Tip: Share CTC only if you are comfortable. “Open to discuss based on the role” is a fine answer.

    Applying for a job seen on WhatsApp or Instagram

    Many Kerala openings are shared in WhatsApp groups and Instagram job pages. Mention where you saw it and the exact post details.

    Apply
    Subject 
    Application — [Job Title] — [Your Name] (via [WhatsApp / Instagram] post)
    Body 
    Dear Hiring Team,

I saw your opening for [Job Title] at [Company] in a job post shared on [WhatsApp / Instagram — page or group name] on [date]. I would like to apply.

I am based in [City] and have [fresher / N years] of experience in [field]. [One line that matches the post — e.g. "I have used Tally and GST billing daily for two years."]

My resume is attached for your review.

Phone: [phone]
Email: [email]

Thank you for considering my application.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: Check the email address matches the company’s real website before sending. Forwarded posts are sometimes fake.

    Applying after a career gap

    You took time off for studies, family, health or caregiving. Name the gap in one line and move straight to why you fit.

    Apply
    Subject 
    Application — [Job Title] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Manager / Hiring Team],

I am applying for the [Job Title] role at [Company]. I have [N] years of experience in [field], most recently as [last role] at [last company].

From [month year] to [month year] I took a planned break for [family / higher studies / health]. During this time I kept my skills current through [course, certification, freelance work or project].

What I bring to this role:
- [Proof line 1 that matches the posting]
- [Proof line 2]

I can join [immediately / from date]. My resume is attached.

Phone: [phone]
Email: [email]

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: One honest sentence about the gap is enough. Don’t apologise for it.

    Returning to work after a break (restart)

    For women’s restart programmes and returnship roles at IT parks and large companies.

    Apply
    Subject 
    Restart programme application — [Job Title] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Team],

I would like to apply for the [restart / returnship programme name] at [Company] for the [Job Title] track.

Before my break I worked for [N] years as [role] at [company], where I [one concrete achievement]. I am now ready to return to full-time work and have recently completed [course or refresher, e.g. "an AWS Cloud Practitioner course"].

I am based in [City] and can work [office / hybrid / remote] from [date].

My resume is attached. Thank you for running programmes that welcome people back.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[phone] · [email]

    Tip: Lead with the work you did before the break. It’s your strongest proof.

    Part-time or freelance role

    For part-time, contract or project work. State your hours and rate expectations clearly.

    Apply
    Subject 
    Part-time [Job Title] — [Your Name] — available [N] hours/week
    Body 
    Dear [Name / Hiring Team],

I am interested in the part-time [Job Title] opportunity at [Company].

Availability: [N] hours a week, [weekdays after 6 pm / weekends / flexible]
Working mode: [remote / on-site in City]
Relevant work: [one or two past projects with links]
Rate: [₹ per hour / per project, or "open to discuss"]

I can start on [date] and am happy to do a short paid trial task.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[phone] · [email]

    Tip: Add one portfolio or sample link. It answers most questions before they ask.

    Relocating to Kerala (Gulf or abroad returnee)

    Moving back from the Gulf or another country. Say when you land and when you can attend interviews in person.

    Apply
    Subject 
    Application — [Job Title] — [Your Name] (relocating to [City], [month])
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Manager / Hiring Team],

I am applying for the [Job Title] position at [Company]. I am currently working as [current role] at [company] in [country] and am relocating to [City] in [month year].

Highlights:
- [N] years in [field], including [region / industry experience]
- [Achievement with a number]
- [Tool, licence or certification relevant to the post]

I am available for online interviews now and in-person meetings from [date]. Notice period: [N days].

My resume is attached.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[phone with country code] · [email]

    Tip: Give an Indian number too if you have one. HR teams find it easier to call.

    Resending: forgot to attach my resume

    You hit send without the attachment. Reply to your own email within minutes. It happens to everyone.

    Follow-up
    Subject 
    Re: Application — [Job Title] — [Your Name] (resume attached)
    Body 
    Dear [Hiring Team],

Apologies, my earlier email for the [Job Title] role was missing my resume. Please find it attached now.

Thank you for your understanding.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[phone]

    Tip: Reply in the same thread so everything stays together. Don’t start a new email.

    Confirm an interview

    HR has shared a date and time. Confirm in one short reply and check the details back.

    Interview
    Subject 
    Re: Interview — [Job Title] — confirmed
    Body 
    Dear [Name],

Thank you for inviting me to interview for the [Job Title] role. I confirm my attendance:

Date: [day, date]
Time: [time]
Mode: [in person at address / Google Meet / Teams / phone]

[If in person:] Please let me know if I should carry any documents or ID.
[If online:] I will join from a quiet place with a stable connection.

Looking forward to speaking with you.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[phone]

    Tip: Repeating the date and time back catches mistakes early.

    Request to reschedule an interview

    Something genuine clashes with the slot. Ask early, give a reason in a few words, and offer options.

    Interview
    Subject 
    Request to reschedule — [Job Title] interview — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Name],

Thank you for scheduling my interview for the [Job Title] role on [date] at [time].

Unfortunately I am unable to attend at that time because of [brief reason, e.g. "a university exam"]. I am very keen on this role and would be grateful for another slot.

I am available on:
- [Option 1: day, date, time]
- [Option 2: day, date, time]
- [Option 3: day, date, time]

Sorry for the inconvenience, and thank you for understanding.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[phone]

    Tip: Ask at least 24 hours before the interview. Three options make it easy to say yes.

    Ask for feedback after a rejection

    You weren’t selected. A gracious reply keeps the door open and sometimes gets useful feedback.

    Interview
    Subject 
    Thank you — [Job Title] interview — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Name],

Thank you for letting me know about your decision for the [Job Title] role. I appreciated the chance to meet the team and learn about [Company].

If you have a moment, I would value any feedback on how I could improve: for example, any skill or area where I fell short.

I would be glad to be considered for future openings that match my profile.

Best wishes,
[Your Name]

    Tip: Many companies can’t share feedback. Thank them either way. Recruiters remember polite candidates.

    Sending documents HR asked for

    After an interview, HR asks for certificates, ID or payslips. Name every file clearly in one email.

    Interview
    Subject 
    Documents for [Job Title] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Name],

As requested, please find the following documents attached:

1. Updated resume
2. [Degree certificate / marks list]
3. [Experience letter / relieving letter]
4. [Last 3 months’ payslips]
5. [ID proof — Aadhaar / PAN]

Please let me know if anything else is needed.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[phone]

    Tip: Name files like “YourName-Payslip-Aug.pdf”. Mask the first 8 digits of Aadhaar unless asked otherwise.

    Accept a job offer

    You have the offer and want to accept. Confirm the key terms in writing.

    Offer
    Subject 
    Offer acceptance — [Job Title] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Name],

Thank you for offering me the position of [Job Title] at [Company]. I am pleased to accept.

As discussed, I confirm:
- Joining date: [date]
- Annual CTC: [amount]
- Work location: [City / office]

I have signed and attached the offer letter. Please let me know what documents I should bring on my first day.

I look forward to joining the team.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[phone]

    Tip: Read the offer letter fully before signing, especially notice period, bond and probation clauses.

    Negotiate salary politely

    The offer is good but below what you expected. Thank them, give one reason and one clear number.

    Offer
    Subject 
    Re: Offer — [Job Title] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Name],

Thank you for the offer for the [Job Title] role. I am excited about joining [Company] and the work on [team or product].

Based on my [N] years of experience in [skill area] and the current market for this role, I was hoping for a CTC of [amount]. [Optional: "My current CTC is X, and I have another offer at Y."]

Is there flexibility to revise the offer closer to this figure? I am also open to discussing [joining bonus / review after 6 months / other benefits].

Thank you for considering this.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: Ask once, politely, with one number. Never mention an offer you don’t actually have.

    Decline a job offer

    You’ve decided to say no. Do it quickly and kindly so they can move to the next candidate.

    Offer
    Subject 
    [Job Title] offer — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Name],

Thank you very much for offering me the [Job Title] position at [Company], and for your time during the process.

After careful thought, I have decided not to accept the offer, as I have chosen a role that is a closer fit for my current goals.

I really enjoyed meeting the team and hope our paths cross again.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: No need to name the other company or share its salary.

    Ask to move the joining date

    You need more time because of notice period, exams or relocation. Ask early, give a new date and the reason.

    Offer
    Subject 
    Joining date — [Job Title] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Name],

Thank you again for the offer for the [Job Title] role. I am looking forward to joining.

My current employer has confirmed my last working day as [date], so I would like to request moving my joining date from [original date] to [new date].

If an earlier date is important for the team, I will check whether my notice period can be shortened.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[phone]

    Tip: Ask as soon as you know. Last-minute requests worry employers.

    Ask for the salary breakup in an offer

    The offer gives only a total CTC. Ask for the monthly in-hand amount and what’s fixed versus variable before you decide.

    Offer
    Subject 
    Query on offer — [Job Title] — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Name],

Thank you for the offer for the [Job Title] role.

Before I confirm, could you please share the salary breakup? In particular:
- Fixed pay and any variable or performance pay
- Expected monthly in-hand salary
- PF, gratuity and insurance contributions
- Any joining bonus, bond or notice-period terms

This will help me respond quickly. Thank you.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: In-hand pay can be much lower than CTC. Always ask before accepting.

    Resignation email

    Short, polite and clear. Thank your manager, state your last working day and offer a smooth handover.

    Resignation
    Subject 
    Resignation — [Your Name] — [Job Title]
    Body 
    Dear [Manager’s Name],

Please accept this email as formal notice of my resignation from my role as [Job Title] at [Company]. As per my notice period of [N days], my last working day will be [date].

Thank you for the support and learning over the past [duration]. I am grateful for [one specific thing].

I will make sure my work is handed over smoothly and will document [projects or tasks] before I leave.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: Tell your manager in person or on a call first, then send this email.

    Request an early release from notice period

    Your new company wants you sooner. Ask your manager for a shorter notice period with a handover plan.

    Resignation
    Subject 
    Request for early release — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [Manager’s Name],

Further to my resignation dated [date], I would like to request an early release, with my last working day on [new date] instead of [original date].

My new employer has asked me to join by [date]. To make sure nothing is left pending, I will:
- Complete [task / project] by [date]
- Hand over [responsibilities] to [colleague]
- Document [processes, credentials, files]

I am also willing to [adjust leave balance / buy out the remaining days] if that helps.

Thank you for considering my request.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: A clear handover plan is what usually gets an early release approved.

    Request relieving letter and experience certificate

    You’ve left and need your documents for the new job. Ask HR politely and list exactly what you need.

    Resignation
    Subject 
    Request for relieving letter — [Your Name] — Employee ID [ID]
    Body 
    Dear HR Team,

I worked at [Company] as [Job Title] from [joining date] to [last working day] (Employee ID: [ID]).

Could you please share the following documents:
1. Relieving letter
2. Experience certificate
3. Full and final settlement statement
4. Form 16 for [financial year]

My new employer needs these by [date]. Please let me know if anything is pending from my side.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[phone] · [personal email]

    Tip: Give your personal email, as your office email may already be closed.

    Request internship completion certificate

    Your internship is ending. Ask for the certificate and, if it went well, a short recommendation.

    Internship
    Subject 
    Internship certificate — [Your Name] — [Team]
    Body 
    Dear [Mentor / HR Name],

My internship as [Intern Title] in the [Team] team ends on [date]. Thank you for the guidance and the chance to work on [project].

Could you please issue my internship completion certificate, mentioning my role, dates and the project I worked on?

If you are comfortable, a short note on my work, or agreeing to be a reference, would mean a lot as I apply for full-time roles.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[College, course] · [phone]

    Tip: Ask a week before your last day, while your work is fresh in their mind.

    Ask HR about your application status (campus drive)

    You attended a campus or pool drive and haven’t heard back. One polite check-in after the promised date.

    Follow-up
    Subject 
    Status of application — [Company] campus drive — [Your Name]
    Body 
    Dear [HR Name / Recruitment Team],

I attended the [Company] recruitment drive at [College / venue] on [date] for the [Job Title] role and cleared [aptitude / technical / HR] round.

I wanted to ask politely whether there is an update on the final results, as I am finalising my plans for [month].

Registration ID: [ID]
College: [college name]
Phone: [phone]

Thank you for your time.

Regards,
[Your Name]

    Tip: Include your registration or roll number so HR can find you in seconds.

    LinkedIn connection note to a hiring manager

    LinkedIn connection notes are limited to about 300 characters. Say why you’re reaching out in two lines.

    Cold outreach
    Subject 
    (LinkedIn connection note, no subject)
    Body 
    Hi [Name], I saw [Company]’s opening for [Job Title] and I’m a [your role] with [N years / a project] in [skill]. I’ve applied and would value connecting to learn more about the team. Thank you!

    Tip: Apply first, then connect. Don’t paste your resume into the note.

    Email to a college senior or alumnus for advice

    A senior from your college works where you want to work. Ask for advice first, not a job.

    Referral
    Subject 
    [College] junior — quick advice on [Company / field]
    Body 
    Hi [Name],

I’m [Your Name], a [year / recent graduate] from [college, course]. I found your profile through [LinkedIn / alumni group] and saw you work as [their role] at [Company].

I’m preparing for [type of role] roles and would really value 15 minutes of your advice on [one or two specific questions, e.g. "which skills the team looks for in freshers"].

If you’re open to it, I can call at a time that suits you. If there’s ever an opening you think I’d fit, I’d be grateful for a referral, but your advice alone would help a lot.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[phone] · [LinkedIn URL]

    Tip: Specific questions get replies. “Any openings?” usually doesn’t.

    Ask about an internship with no posted opening

    Small companies and startups often take interns without advertising. Show one piece of work and your dates.

    Internship
    Subject 
    Internship request — [Field] — [Your Name], [College]
    Body 
    Dear [Name / Team],

I’m a [year] [course] student at [college] and I’d like to ask whether [Company] takes interns in [field] between [start date] and [end date].

I recently [built / completed] [project or work] — [link]. I’m comfortable with [two or three tools or skills].

I can work [full-time / part-time] [in City / remotely] and am happy to start with a small task so you can see my work.

Thank you for your time.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[phone] · [email]

    Tip: One working link beats a long list of skills.

    Elements of an email application

    Six parts. Keep each one short.

    1
    Subject line

    [Your Name] — [Job Title]

    2
    Salutation

    Dear [Hiring Manager / Recruiter name],

    3
    First paragraph

    Who you are, exact role, where you found it, and your city.

    4
    Second paragraph

    One or two proof lines that match the posting.

    5
    Third paragraph

    Thank them and offer a call if useful.

    6
    Closing

    Regards, / Sincerely,
    [Name] · [Phone] · [Email]

    Before you hit send

    • Mail only the address on the official posting. Check the company domain looks real.
    • Use the exact job title in the subject and first line.
    • Send a separate email per role — do not mix “any open position” into one mail.
    • Never pay for a job or interview. Never send OTP, Aadhaar, or bank details.
