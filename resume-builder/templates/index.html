FAQ

Are these resume templates free?

Yes. All 167 designs are free to preview, edit, and download as a selectable-text PDF — no watermark, no credit card.

What makes a resume template ATS-friendly?

Clear headings, simple columns, and real text in the PDF so trackers can read your name, email, experience and skills. Avoid scanned images and heavy tables.

Which template should freshers use?

Freshers or ATS-Friendly designs that lead with education, projects and skills. Keep to one page when you can, then run the free ATS checker.

Can I use these for Infopark or Technopark jobs?

Yes — built for India IT and campus hiring. See the Kerala IT resume guide for more detail.
