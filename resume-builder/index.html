01 · READABILITY

Can your content be read?

Review extracted text, contact information and common section headings. If a scanned PDF has no readable text, paste the text or export a text-based PDF from your document editor.

02 · RELEVANCE

Does it reflect the role?

A job description enables keyword comparison: matched terms and missing terms. Include a missing skill only when it honestly reflects your experience. Without a description, the check focuses on the resume itself.

03 · IMPACT

Show what you contributed.

Review weak phrasing and suggested bullet improvements. Describe your action, the tool or method, and the result. Add real numbers where you have evidence; never invent achievements to improve a score.

Understanding your report

What does the score mean?

It is a guide based on this tool’s text checks and keyword matching. It is not an employer’s ATS score, an interview probability or a guarantee of passing screening. Use the individual findings to decide what to improve.

Which files can I check?

Use PDF, DOCX or TXT files up to 5 MB, or paste your resume text. Image-only scans may not contain extractable text. Text extraction cannot reliably confirm every visual layout issue.

Do I need an account, and is my resume saved?

No login is needed. This checker processes your resume in your browser and does not upload or save the resume to our servers. Reloading clears your report. Resume Builder account storage is a separate feature.

How should I use the suggestions?

Fix missing contact details and unclear sections first. Then check relevant skills and improve experience bullets. Edit your source resume, export it again and rerun the check against the same job description.
