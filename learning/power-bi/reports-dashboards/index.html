Building reports & dashboards

Choosing visuals, KPI cards, slicers, drill-down, tooltips and good report design.

← CALCULATE & time intelligence Publishing, refresh & security →

A report should answer its audience's questions in seconds. A layout that works for most business reports:

  1. Top row — KPI cards: Total Sales, Orders, Avg Order, YoY Growth %.
  2. Middle — trend over time (line chart) and breakdown by category or region (bar chart).
  3. Bottom — a detail table or matrix for people who need the numbers.
  4. Slicers — Date range, Region, Category, placed consistently.

Choosing visuals

  • Comparison → bar or column · Trend → line · Part of a whole → stacked bar (pie only with 2–4 slices) · Relationship → scatter · Single number → card.
  • Add drill-down (Year → Month → Day) and tooltips for detail without clutter.
  • Use few colours, highlight what matters, and label everything clearly.

Key points

  • Lead with KPI cards, then trends, then detail.
  • Match the visual to the question: bar, line, card, matrix.
  • Slicers and cross-filtering make reports interactive.
  • Keep colours few and labels clear.

Quick check

Which visual is best for showing sales trend across 12 months?

Line charts show change over time most clearly; pies suit a few parts of a whole.

Practice task

Build a one-page sales report with four KPI cards, a monthly trend line with last year's line, a sorted bar chart by region and a Date slicer, then ask a friend what they learn from it in 10 seconds.

Interview corner

How do you design an effective dashboard?

Start from the audience's key questions, put the main KPIs at the top, show trends and breakdowns next, keep detail tables at the bottom, use consistent slicers and colours, avoid clutter and make sure the numbers reconcile with the source.

What is drill-down versus drill-through?

Drill-down moves down a hierarchy inside the same visual (Year → Month → Day). Drill-through jumps to a separate detail page filtered to the item you right-clicked, such as one customer's full history.
