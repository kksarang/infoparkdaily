A one-page fresher resume that passes ATS

The sections, order and wording that both software filters and recruiters read easily.

← PreviousHow fresher hiring works Next lesson →LinkedIn and job-portal profiles recruiters find

Your resume is read twice: first by an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) that extracts text and matches keywords, then by a recruiter who spends well under a minute on it. Design for both.

Section order for freshers

  1. Header — name, phone, email, city, LinkedIn and GitHub links as plain text.
  2. Summary — two or three lines: degree, target role, three to five key skills.
  3. Skills — a plain comma-separated list grouped by type (Languages, Frameworks, Tools).
  4. Projects — your strongest section as a fresher: what you built, your role, the tech used and one result.
  5. Internships / experience — even short ones count.
  6. Education — degree, college, year, and marks if they are good.
  7. Certifications / achievements — only relevant ones.

Rules that keep the ATS happy

  • One column, standard headings, no tables, text boxes or skill bars.
  • Save as a text-based PDF — never a scanned image.
  • Copy exact tool names from the job post when they are true for you ("React", not "ReactJS framework knowledge").
  • Skip photo, date of birth, father's name, religion and a "Declaration" — they waste space.

Worked example

Adapt it to your situation
PROJECTS

Job Tracker Web App                        React, Node.js, MongoDB
• Built the React frontend and login API for a 3-member team project.
• Added search and filters so users find a saved job in under 5 seconds.
• Used by 40 classmates during placement season.

Weak version of the same project:
• Did a project on job tracking using MERN stack. Worked on frontend.

What to notice

• Strong bullets start with an action verb (Built, Added, Used by).
• They name the exact tools — the ATS can match them to a job post.
• They show your personal part in a team project.
• One number (40 users, 5 seconds) makes the work concrete.

Names, companies and numbers are fictional examples. Always confirm details on the employer's official site.

Key points

  • One page, one column, text PDF.
  • Projects are the core section for freshers.
  • Mirror exact tool names from the job post — only if true.
  • Remove personal details and declarations.

Quick check

Which layout is safest for ATS parsing?

ATS software reads text top to bottom; columns, tables and images often scramble or hide your content.

Practice task

Rewrite your two best project bullets using: action verb + what you built + tools + one result. Then run your resume against a real job post in the ATS checker.

Interview corner

Walk me through your resume.

Go in order — education, projects, internship — spending most time on the one most relevant to this job, with your role and one result.

Why is this skill on your resume? Tell me how you used it.

Only list skills you can discuss. Answer with a specific project or task where you used it and what you learned.
