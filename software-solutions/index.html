06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

Have something else in mind?
Let’s talk about it ↗

Can you build both Android and iOS apps?+

Yes. We plan mobile apps for Android and iOS, choosing the approach around the features, device requirements, and maintenance needs of your product.

Can you improve our existing software?+

Yes. We start by reviewing the code, current workflows, and pain points, then agree which fixes or improvements make sense.

Can we start with an MVP?+

Yes. We can define a focused first version that tests the core idea, with a clear list of what to build now and what can wait.

Can you integrate our current business tools?+

We can assess your tools’ APIs, permissions, and data requirements, then plan the integrations that are technically available.

Do you provide design and video services separately?+

Yes. Product design, branding, graphics, video, and digital support can be scoped as individual engagements or alongside a software build.

How are project cost and timeline determined?+

We review the scope, complexity, integrations, content, and review process before proposing a budget and milestones. Estimates are agreed around the actual requirements.

What happens after launch?+

Handover and any ongoing maintenance, monitoring, updates, or support are agreed as part of the engagement. Support scope and response expectations are documented.

How are source code ownership and handover handled?+

Scope, source code ownership, third-party licences, access, handover materials, and support terms are documented in the project agreement before work begins.
