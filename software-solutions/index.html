09 / HOW WE WORK

Clear milestones.
Visible progress.
A practical handover.

Weekly reviews and milestone reporting keep priorities, decisions, and the next release visible.

  1. Discovery & scope

    Understand the business need, users, constraints, and first-release priorities.

  2. Product design & architecture

    Define the experience, technical approach, and acceptance criteria.

  3. Development & review

    Build in milestones and review working features together.

  4. QA & acceptance

    Test agreed workflows and resolve issues against the acceptance criteria.

  5. Launch & stabilization

    Deploy the agreed release and address launch-stage issues.

  6. Documentation & handover

    Provide agreed repository access, deployment guidance, secure admin access transfer, walkthroughs, and maintenance notes.

  7. Ongoing improvement

    Plan enhancements and document ongoing support responsibilities in the service agreement.
