Questions recruiters ask

Does submitting publish the job immediately?

No. Every request is reviewed. It goes on Jobs only after we verify the company and HR contact.

Do I pay InfoparkDaily to list?

Never pay to list through InfoparkDaily, and never ask candidates to pay. Ignore anyone requesting a listing fee in our name.

How long does review take?

We review each request ourselves. Timing depends on volume. If something is missing, we contact the HR phone or work email you entered.

How do I track a request?

After submit you get a request ID. Open Track a request and look it up in this browser, or email us with the company name.

Can I post walk-ins or many roles at once?

Yes. Choose Walk-in drive or Mass hiring above, then add extra roles in the form. We confirm details before any campaign goes out.

Recruit rules & awareness Verify first · no fake listings · never pay

InfoparkDaily is a community help page for job seekers — not Infopark management, not company HR, and not a recruitment agency. Submitting here does not auto-publish on Jobs.

  • Verify first — we check company, role, HR phone, and contacts before publishing.
  • Fake requests — incomplete, misleading, or unverifiable openings are not listed.
  • Truthful only — share only real openings you are authorised to post.
  • Never pay — no candidate or “listing” fees via InfoparkDaily.
  • Full conditions live in Privacy and Terms. Issues: Contact.
