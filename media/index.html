Who we are

An independent community — not Infopark official

01

Who we are

InfoparkDaily is Kerala’s biggest independent IT community family — started by Sarang R. Not Infopark Kerala, not Technopark, not Cyberpark. InfoparkDaily Media and InfoparkDaily Jobs are the same family.

02

What we do

Daily openings across Infopark Kochi, Technopark Trivandrum, and Cyberpark — plus park news and original career guides. Brands can run campaigns with us; companies share roles through Recruit.

03

How jobs are sourced

Listings come from public park portals, company sites, LinkedIn, social posts, and reviewed Recruit submissions. Always re-check the official page before applying. We never charge a candidate fee.
