01

Who we are

InfoparkDaily is Kerala’s biggest independent IT community family — started by Sarang R. Not Infopark Kerala, not Technopark, not Cyberpark, and not any official organisation. InfoparkDaily Media and InfoparkDaily Jobs are the same family. We are not a government body, park authority, or hiring company.

02

What we do

The largest daily reach for openings across Infopark Kochi, Technopark Trivandrum, and Cyberpark — plus park news and original career guides. Brands run campaigns with us; companies share roles through Recruit. Enitexa.ai builds this website as our software partner — also independent.

03

How jobs are sourced

Openings come from outside public sources — park portals, company sites, LinkedIn, social posts, community leads, and reviewed Recruit submissions. We cannot guarantee every listing is genuine or still open. Always re-check the employer or official park page before applying. InfoparkDaily is not the employer and never charges a candidate fee.
