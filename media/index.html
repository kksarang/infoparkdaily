Who we are

InfoparkDaily is Kerala’s biggest independent IT community family — an Instagram community started by Sarang R, not Infopark Kerala, not Technopark, not Cyberpark, and not any official organisation. InfoparkDaily Media and InfoparkDaily Jobs are the same family. 50K+ families follow this reach for jobs, news, and campus life. We are not a government body, park authority, or hiring company.

What we do

We run the largest daily reach for openings across Infopark Kochi, Technopark Trivandrum, and Cyberpark — plus park news and original career guides on applying safely, walk-ins, and fresher prep. Brands can run campaigns with us; companies can share roles through Recruit. Enitexa (Enitexa.ai) is our freelance software partner for the website — it is not Infopark official either.

How jobs are sourced

Openings come from outside public sources — park portals, company sites, LinkedIn, social posts, community leads, and reviewed Recruit submissions. Poster claims are not treated as proof of hiring. We cannot guarantee every listing is genuine or still open. Always re-check the employer or official park page before applying or travelling. InfoparkDaily is not the employer and never charges a candidate fee.
