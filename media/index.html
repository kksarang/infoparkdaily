About Us

From a page to Kerala’s tech community

InfoparkDaily began as a simple social page sharing Infopark updates. It grew into a real community, then a website, and today a daily job portal for Kerala’s IT parks — still independent, still free to follow, and still built for people who work and hire here.

Who we are

InfoparkDaily is an individual Instagram community page started by Sarang R — not Infopark Kerala, not Technopark, not Cyberpark, and not any official organisation. Related community handles such as InfoparkDaily Media and InfoparkDaily Jobs are part of the same independent page family. We publish community news, career guides, and curated job digests to help youngsters and job seekers — we are not a government body, park authority, or hiring company.

What we do

Every day we surface openings across Infopark Kochi, Technopark Trivandrum, and Cyberpark, share park news, and publish original career guides on applying safely, walk-ins, and fresher prep. Brands can run campaigns with us; companies can share roles through Recruit. Enitexa (Enitexa.ai) is our freelance software / digital service provider for website and tech support — it is not Infopark official either. Reach us anytime via Contact.

How jobs are sourced

We collect openings from outside sources — Infopark and Technopark portals, company sites, LinkedIn, social media, individuals who share leads, and openings submitted through Recruit (which we review before publishing). Poster claims alone are not treated as proof of hiring. We cannot guarantee every listing is 100% genuine or still correct — visitors must strictly re-check the employer or official park listing before applying or travelling. InfoparkDaily is not the employer, never charges candidates a fee, and is not responsible for outcomes if someone skips verification. See Terms and Privacy.

    Now we’re a job portal
