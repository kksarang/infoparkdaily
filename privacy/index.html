1. Scope

This policy covers the InfoparkDaily website (including Jobs, Recruit, Job details, News, Contact, and related pages) and messages you send us voluntarily. It does not control Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Maps, or employer career portals — those services have their own policies.

2. Job listing data (outside / public sources)

InfoparkDaily started to help and support youngsters and job seekers. We publish job and internship information gathered from outside sources — for example Infopark and Technopark job sites, company websites, careers pages, LinkedIn, social media, individuals who share leads, and other public channels — plus company requests reviewed via Recruit. We do this so Kerala’s IT community can discover opportunities more easily.

  • We are not the employer. We do not receive your resume for company hiring unless you choose to email our community inbox for a separate support reason.
  • Because listings are compiled from outside websites, people, and social channels, we cannot guarantee that every opportunity is genuine, original, or still correct. Anyone who visits this site and uses the data must strictly verify on official company or park channels before applying, travelling, sharing documents, or paying anyone.
  • Never pay any amount for a job or interview. InfoparkDaily never charges candidates.
  • When a detail cannot be verified, our pages may show “Not officially disclosed by the company.” — we do not invent contacts or salaries.
  • If any issue arises after you use community information, InfoparkDaily is not responsible for that outcome. Full liability rules are in our Terms. Final verification is always your responsibility.

3. Recruit — hiring request data

When a company or authorised contact uses Recruit (or Contact / email for the same purpose), they send us hiring details so we can consider listing an opening on Jobs.

  • We may collect name, email or phone, company name, location, role, vacancy details, and any message text you provide.
  • We use this data to review the request, attempt verification, communicate about the request, and — only if accepted — publish a public Jobs listing for job seekers.
  • Requests are not published automatically. Fake, incomplete, misleading, or unverifiable requests are not added to Jobs.
  • Published listing fields may include company, role, location, and apply contacts that the submitter asked us to share. Do not send passwords, OTPs, or candidate payment details through Recruit.
  • Full Recruit rules and liability are also in our Terms. We may update Privacy and Terms as new community needs or issues arise.

4. What we may collect

  • Contact / Recruit / email / WhatsApp / Instagram DMs: name, email or phone, company name (if you provide it), and your message (job post request, campaign brief, support request, etc.).
  • Technical basics: standard web server / browser behaviour may include IP address and device type if hosting logs them. We do not use this to build marketing profiles.
  • Local preference only: theme choice (ipd-theme) and optional UI flags (for example dismissing the jobs alert bar) stored in your browser’s localStorage.
  • We do not ask candidates to upload resumes to InfoparkDaily to apply for jobs. Applications go to company portals.

5. How we use information

  • To reply to your inquiry, Recruit request, or collaboration request.
  • To review and verify company hiring requests before deciding whether to publish on Jobs.
  • To correct or remove a listing if you report an issue.
  • To review reports about fee requests, false statements, fake Recruit posts, or other safety concerns related to a job you saw on InfoparkDaily.
  • To operate and improve the community website experience for job seekers.
  • We do not sell personal data. We do not charge candidates for job alerts.

6. Storage, cookies & caching

  • The site may use a service worker to cache public pages for faster loading. Cached files are public site assets, not your private messages.
  • Theme and small UI preferences stay on your device until you clear site data. These are treated as essential / functional storage.
  • Contact messages sent by email / Formspree (if enabled) or WhatsApp are processed only to respond to you.
  • Your cookie / analytics choice is stored locally (for example ipd_consent_v1) so we remember Accept or Reject on later visits.

7. Analytics, cookies & consent

If you Accept cookies, we may load Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity, and Google AdSense to understand traffic, improve pages, and show advertisements. Clarity may review anonymized session replays, click/scroll heatmaps, and interaction signals such as dead or rage clicks. Contact form fields are masked in Clarity recordings where possible.

  • We use Google Consent Mode v2. Until you Accept, advertising and analytics storage signals default to denied. After Accept, analytics and advertising storage may be granted so measurement and AdSense can work as configured.
  • If you choose Essential only in Cookie Settings, we do not enable Microsoft Clarity or AdSense, and Google Analytics remains restricted under denied analytics storage (no analytics cookies for measurement as configured).
  • You can change your mind anytime via or by clearing site data in your browser.
  • Third-party privacy info: Google Privacy Policy · Microsoft Privacy Statement.

8. Third-party links

Job “Apply” links open employer websites. Maps, Instagram embeds, and WhatsApp are third-party services. Before you submit personal data on those sites, read their privacy policies. InfoparkDaily is not responsible for third-party data practices.

9. Report fee requests, false information & financial issues

If anyone asks for money for a job, shares a false statement, or causes a financial / document safety concern related to content you found on InfoparkDaily, please connect our team. We can check the issue and update or remove content where appropriate.

  • Use the Contact page, email infoparkstorieskochi@gmail.com, or call / WhatsApp +91 99952 54290.
  • Share the job link / company name and what happened. Do not send OTPs, passwords, or payment credentials to anyone.
  • InfoparkDaily never charges candidates. We clarify this so our community stays safer.

Also read our Terms — Report issues and Terms — Verify before confirm.

10. Your choices

  • You can Accept all or choose Essential only via the site banner / Cookie settings.
  • You can request correction or removal of community content you believe is wrong or harmful.
  • You can ask us to delete contact-form correspondence we still hold, where practical.
  • You can clear browser storage / site data anytime in your browser settings.
  • Contact: infoparkstorieskochi@gmail.com or Contact page.

11. Children

Our content is aimed at students and working professionals seeking IT jobs and career updates. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it where possible.

12. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy and related Terms as community needs or new issues arise (including Recruit and Jobs safety rules). The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after updates means you accept the revised policy.
